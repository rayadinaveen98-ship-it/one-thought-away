# One Thought Away

**Original animated short film — pre-production**

> A five-year-old girl discovers she can go anywhere with a thought. Then she tries to reach the one person she misses most.

## Current status
- **Project:** One Thought Away
- **Stage:** Pre-production — Anu reference control and feasibility planning
- **Lead:** Anu (age 5)
- **Story setting:** A warm, lived-in South Indian family world
- **Working runtime:** 8–9 minutes (proposal, not locked)
- **Recommended art direction:** painterly cinematic 3D, tactile storybook sensibility (approval pending)
- **Pipeline target:** Blender 5.3; 24 fps; 1080p working format (technical feasibility not yet tested)

## Story promise
Magic begins as a child's playful delight. The emotional turn must come **after** the adventures, when Anu accidentally finds Amma's photograph and discovers her power cannot bring Amma back. Her Mammaya helps her rediscover memories. The film must remain emotionally honest, gentle, and child-centered.

## Production rules
1. Preserve story chronology and established character identity.
2. Mark every new artistic or technical choice as **proposed** until approved.
3. Get visual approval on design sheets before modeling and final animation.
4. Test Blender scene complexity and render costs before committing to production.
5. Keep large render caches outside ordinary Git. Record third-party licenses.
