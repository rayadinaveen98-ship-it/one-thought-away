# Story canon — One Thought Away

## Established story foundations (from earlier creative planning)
**Premise:** Anu, a five-year-old girl, watches teleportation in a cartoon and wishes to travel anywhere simply by thinking. Her wish comes true, without a scientific explanation. The film treats the magic as a child's discovery, not a superhero origin story.

## Story chronology to preserve
1. Anu sees teleportation in a cartoon and makes her wish.
2. Next morning, an **ice-cream teleportation** reveals the power.
3. Playful adventures follow, including **surprising Dad at work** and a **guitar moment with Mammaya**.
4. While playing, Anu **accidentally discovers Amma's photograph**.
5. She tries to teleport to Amma, but her attempts do not work.
6. **Mammaya prompts Anu's memories**; do not put the memory sequence earlier.
7. Remembered experiences include dancing, learning rhymes, dressing up, bedtime stories and hide-and-seek.
8. The resolution is about loving memories that remain, rather than magic overcoming death.

## Creative guardrails
- **Protect the reveal:** the joyful first part must not obviously announce that the story is about loss.
- **Amma is not shown fully and clearly as a present on-screen character**. Use fragments of remembered gestures, framing, sound and point of view.
- **Anu is five**; preserve spontaneous, credible child behavior and short, natural dialogue.
- **No missing front tooth** in Anu's design.
- Keep locations grounded in a lived-in **South Indian family environment**.
- No exposition explaining the cause or mechanics of the teleportation.
- Avoid an explicit depiction of death or illness not established by the story.
- Do not invent sidekicks, additional characters or major incidents without approval.

## Separate earlier exploration
A **28-page cinematic graphic novel** and warm European-style illustration direction were explored previously. These may inspire visual development, but the animated film is a distinct adaptation.

## Open decisions
Scene-level actions, exact dialogue, ending shot, final runtime, costumes and sets, detailed memory imagery, magical visual language, audio, and storyboard are not locked.
