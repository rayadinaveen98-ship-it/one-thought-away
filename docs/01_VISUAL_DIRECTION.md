# Visual direction — Proposal v0.1 (not approved)

## Recommended direction
**Painterly cinematic 3D with tactile storybook texture.** Forms are subtly stylized, lighting is natural and emotionally motivated, the environments feel personally inhabited, and the characters avoid plastic surfaces or uncanny photorealism. The illustrated quality should emerge from handcrafted materials, restrained color grading and composition rather than direct imitation of a known film or studio.

## Why this approach
- **Emotion:** convincing faces, body language and unforced pauses support Anu's experience.
- **Wonder:** a five-year-old's eye level makes ordinary rooms feel surprising and magical.
- **Continuity:** real, reusable 3D characters and sets are consistent across camera angles and shots.
- **Feasibility:** a compact set library, simplified surfaces, Eevee tests and purposeful camera language are practical for a small production.
- **Identity:** inherit the warmth of our prior storybook exploration without creating a generic AI-cinema aesthetic.

## Preliminary color and lighting journey
- **Discovery:** honeyed daylight, cream walls, sunlit skin, a few playful yellow/teal accents.
- **Adventures:** brighter color separation and joyful spatial compositions, still rooted in reality.
- **Photo and unanswered wishes:** fewer visual distractions, subdued indirect light, longer quiet holds.
- **Memories and ending:** gentle human warmth; no obligatory blue sadness filter, glowing heavenly figure or dramatic fantasy portal.

## Teleportation — to be tested
Playful, subjective and brief: an almost imperceptible spatial ripple, edit-timed sound shift, or energetic match cut. Avoid sci-fi interfaces, giant luminous portals or mechanics-driven storytelling. Test three treatments before approval.

## Cinematography — draft
- Child-height framing for discovery and movement; wider frames only when dramatically needed.
- Responsive but motivated handheld-feeling movement, not constant camera swoops.
- Hold a close-up when Anu realizes the power doesn't work for Amma.
- Memories favor hands, silhouettes, objects, and incomplete glimpses, preserving the established no-full-Amma rule.

## Required look-dev approvals
1. Anu character sheet: front, side, three-quarter, expression range.
2. One family-home daylight frame.
3. Anu with Mammaya and guitar in the same lighting/material style.
4. A photo-discovery frame, without premature memory images.
5. Teleportation treatment comparisons; short rig/acting test.
