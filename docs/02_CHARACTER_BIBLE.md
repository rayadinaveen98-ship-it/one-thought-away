# Character bible — Working v0.2

## Anu — protagonist
- **Age:** 5 years (established).
- **Identity features:** messy shoulder-length dark hair; **yellow butterfly hair clip**; **no missing front tooth** (established).
- **Behavior:** curious, impulsive, silly, inventive, easily delighted, emotionally direct.
- **Design proposal:** authentic child proportions with a modestly stylized face, expressive brows and eyes, natural teeth, soft hair breakup and convincing small-child posture. Avoid oversized toy eyes or fashion-doll anatomy.
- **Clothes:** ordinary everyday South Indian home clothing; exact outfit/colors **TBD**.
- **Acting:** protect quiet reactions and child-level emotional logic over explanatory dialogue.

## Mammaya — close, playful uncle
- **Story role:** shares a guitar adventure; later gently helps Anu remember Amma (established).
- **Design proposal:** warm, believable young adult with ordinary lived-in clothes and restrained facial acting.
- **Open:** exact age, face, hair, wardrobe, voice and familial details not yet designed.
- **Acting:** attentive and truthful; avoids preaching about grief.

## Dad — supporting
- **Story role:** Anu surprises him at his workplace (established).
- **Open:** profession, workplace setting, age, appearance, costume and amount of dialogue.
- **Acting:** his reaction should come from genuine surprise and affection, not broad slapstick.

## Amma — remembered presence
- **Status:** passed away before the present timeline (established).
- **Framing rule:** do not show her as a fully revealed on-screen character.
- **Memory moments:** dancing, rhymes, dressing up, bedtime stories, hide-and-seek, **only after Mammaya prompts Anu**.
- **Visual options:** hands, fabric, shadow, over-shoulder crops, familiar items, voice fragments and Anu's point of view. These are ideas, not locked shots.

## Casting/rig workflow
For each visible character, approve silhouette, turnaround, 6–8 expression studies, gesture sheet, color/material sheet and rig feasibility before final model. Add no unapproved central characters solely to fill runtime.
