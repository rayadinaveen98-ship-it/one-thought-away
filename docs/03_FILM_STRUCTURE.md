# Film structure — Exploratory beat map

**Target:** 8–9 minutes, to be recalculated after screenplay and animatic. The following is **pacing guidance**, not locked screen timing.

| Section | Approx. window | Narrative intention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening/cartoon/wish | 0:00–1:00 | Establish a normal child's evening and playful imagination |
| Ice-cream discovery | 1:00–2:00 | Reveal power through comedy and delighted surprise |
| Adventures | 2:00–4:15 | Explore Dad-at-work surprise and Mammaya's guitar; establish relationships |
| Photograph | 4:15–4:55 | An unexpected object changes the meaning of the magic |
| Failed teleportation | 4:55–6:15 | Let Anu try with genuine, age-appropriate persistence |
| Mammaya and memories | 6:15–8:05 | Anu's memories emerge only when Mammaya gently invites them |
| Resolution | 8:05–8:50 | Quiet, unsentimental image of memory and continuing love |

## Dramatic structure principles
1. Do not begin with grief exposition or imply a tragic twist during the fantasy opening.
2. Spend enough time with Anu's joy that her disappointment feels like a real loss of possibility.
3. A child is not expected to express an adult philosophical conclusion.
4. The emotional transition should be carried by acting, stillness and sound rather than long speeches.
5. No detailed ending image or final dialogue is approved at this stage.
