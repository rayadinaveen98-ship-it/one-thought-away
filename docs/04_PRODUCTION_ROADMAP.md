## Rendering and storage feasibility
An 8m50s film at 24fps contains **12,720 frames**. This is a significant rendering and animation project; no completion date or render speed is promised without actual tests. Use low-resolution proxy previews before high-quality final renders. Do not commit multi-GB image sequences or caches to ordinary Git.

## Review policy
No silent major story or character redesigns. Changes go through the decision log; each important milestone receives explicit creative approval.
